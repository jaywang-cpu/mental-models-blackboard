想不出理由时 · 健 + 能(6 维度之 3-4)

健 Health
· Regular [exercise / outdoor activity] not only improves physical health but also alleviates stress, thus contributing to overall well-being.
· Given that [fast food / long working hours] is increasingly common, encouraging people to [X] would help them lead a healthier lifestyle.
· If [X] continues, it may place an enormous strain on the healthcare system, which would affect everyone in society.
· By [X], people are less likely to suffer from [obesity / anxiety], which would significantly improve their quality of life.
· Admittedly, [X] may bring short-term pleasure; however, its long-term impact on health cannot be ignored.
能 Skills
· Through [internships / hands-on projects], young people can develop practical skills that classroom learning alone cannot provide.
· Rather than merely memorising facts, students who [X] gain valuable experience in [teamwork / problem-solving].
· This experience would greatly improve their career prospects, since employers increasingly value [communication / adaptability].
· By stepping out of their comfort zone, [人群] are more likely to become independent and confident.
· Such skills are not only useful in the workplace but also equip people to deal with challenges in everyday life.
