7.5 规矩

· 立场从开头到结尾不能变;每段第一句就是这段的观点
· 每段只讲一个理由,讲透:解释 + 例子 比 堆三个理由强
· 连接词每段最多 2 个(你过度用连接词,这会扣连贯分)
· 模板只是骨架,方括号里必须填题目自己的词;整句照搬无关模板,考官会不计分
· 大作文 260-300 词,小作文 160-190 词。拼不准的词换成会拼的,别冒险

1 · 导师

Professor Zheng · 本科三年酶工程实验室 · Part 2 第 1 4 6 7 9 11 27 31 题
开场Well, the person who really springs to mind is Professor Zheng, my undergraduate supervisor, who I met in my very first semester.
特征When I think of him, the thing that stands out most is how organized he is. Every experiment had a plan, every failure had a note.
F 块He is affable and charismatic, and he infects everyone in the lab with his passion. His patience is unparalleled, nobody is on a par with him.
故事I remember one summer our enzyme data got lost after a power cut, and because of that, I had to redo three months of PCR and gel work. He stayed with me every night until 2 a.m.
A 块That year really expanded my skill set. It sharpened my competitive edge and set me apart from others, so later I could gain a foothold at Johns Hopkins.
转折Having said that, he is not perfect. He is brutally honest, and my first draft came back covered in red.
收尾Now that I come to think of it, I probably should have thanked him more often. If only every student had a mentor like him.

· 尊敬的年长者 / 困难成功的人:故事改成「他经费被砍,还是把论文发出来了」
· 印象深刻的课程:主角换成「他的蛋白工程课」,故事不变
· 小时候学的技能:换成「我 5 岁学做饭」,A 块不变
