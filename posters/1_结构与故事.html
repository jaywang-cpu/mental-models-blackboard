7.5 规矩

· 立场从开头到结尾不能变;每段第一句就是这段的观点
· 每段只讲一个理由,讲透:解释 + 例子 比 堆三个理由强
· 连接词每段最多 2 个(你过度用连接词,这会扣连贯分)
· 模板只是骨架,方括号里必须填题目自己的词;整句照搬无关模板,考官会不计分
· 大作文 260-300 词,小作文 160-190 词。拼不准的词换成会拼的,别冒险

想不出理由时 · 钱 + 时(6 维度之 1-2)

钱 Money
· Not only would this [政策/做法] ease the financial burden on [families], but it would also channel more money into [the local economy].
· Although the initial cost of [X] may seem considerable, it should be regarded as a long-term investment rather than an unnecessary expense.
· By [减少/提供 X], governments could free up public funds, which could in turn be spent on [education / healthcare].
· In the long run, the economic returns of [X] are likely to far outweigh the money spent on it.
· Without [X], many [low-income families] would be unable to afford [基本需要], which would further widen the gap between rich and poor.
时 Time
· By [using technology / working from home], people can save a considerable amount of time, thereby allowing them to focus on [what truly matters].
· While [X] may require extra effort at first, it enables people to work far more efficiently in the long run.
· Given that most people already have demanding schedules, [X] would help them strike a better balance between work and [family life].
· Rather than wasting hours on [commuting / paperwork], people could spend that time on [learning new skills].
· As a result, [人群] would be able to complete tasks in a fraction of the time, which would ultimately raise overall productivity.

口语

4 个故事 · 换第一句和主角,中间不动

1 · 导师

Professor Zheng · 本科三年酶工程实验室 · Part 2 第 1 4 6 7 9 11 27 31 题
开场Well, the person who really springs to mind is Professor Zheng, my undergraduate supervisor, who I met in my very first semester.
特征When I think of him, the thing that stands out most is how organized he is. Every experiment had a plan, every failure had a note.
F 块He is affable and charismatic, and he infects everyone in the lab with his passion. His patience is unparalleled, nobody is on a par with him.
故事I remember one summer our enzyme data got lost after a power cut, and because of that, I had to redo three months of PCR and gel work. He stayed with me every night until 2 a.m.
A 块That year really expanded my skill set. It sharpened my competitive edge and set me apart from others, so later I could gain a foothold at Johns Hopkins.
转折Having said that, he is not perfect. He is brutally honest, and my first draft came back covered in red.
收尾Now that I come to think of it, I probably should have thanked him more often. If only every student had a mentor like him.
· 教你新技能的人:故事改成「他教我做 PCR,第一次失败了五遍」
· 有条理的人:把「特征」那句拉长,讲他的实验记录本
· 尊敬的年长者 / 困难成功的人:故事改成「他经费被砍,还是把论文发出来了」
· 印象深刻的课程:主角换成「他的蛋白工程课」,故事不变
· 小时候学的技能:换成「我 5 岁学做饭」,A 块不变
· 重要决定:「大一第一学期进实验室」就是那个决定

2 · 合伙人

Yvette · MAITONG 联合创始人 · Part 2 第 2 5 10 13 15 21 题
开场Funny I should get this topic. I'm probably one of the least social people in the world, but I do know someone who is, my co-founder Yvette.
F 块She is such a larger than life character. She always has a story to tell, and she infects everyone with her optimism.
相识We met at a startup event at Hopkins. She didn't make much of an impression on me at first, but the more we talked about Chinese medicine and AI, the more I wanted to know her.
故事I remember one evening we sat in a Baltimore café until it closed, and because of that conversation, we decided to start MAITONG, an AI tool for pulse diagnosis.
J 块Working with her is a great bonding experience. We shoot the breeze, share anecdotes, and the whole atmosphere is vibrant and cordial.
转折Having said that, starting a company is not easy. I saved money for a year and skipped a lot of dinners out just to pay for our first server.
收尾I wouldn't say she changed my life, but she definitely changed how I look at teamwork. After two years, I can't imagine building anything without her.
· 快乐人士 / 受欢迎的人:F 块多说两句,故事不变
· 帮助他人:故事改成「我帮她把技术架构从零搭起来」
· 攒钱买特别物品:物品 = 第一台服务器,转折那句拉成故事
· 重要决定:决定 = 开公司,收尾用「I'd do it again」
· Part 3 公司题:直接讲 MAITONG,政府题用 H 块

3 · 巴尔的摩

Inner Harbor · Peabody Library · DC · Part 2 第 17 22 23 24 25 26 28 34 题
开场Well, one place that's always stuck in my mind is the Inner Harbor in Baltimore, about a 15-minute bus ride from my apartment.
I 块I can feast my eyes on the landscape there. It prides itself on the stunning waterfront, sparkling water and radiant sunshine, a picturesque backdrop for photos and leisurely strolls.
故事I remember last Fourth of July, five of us from the lab went there for the fireworks. On the way we got caught in the crowd, so by the time we found a spot, we could barely move.
J 块But we gathered together and enjoyed each other's company. We shot the breeze, shared anecdotes, and the ambience was electrifying, bustling with laughter.
C 块After a week in the lab I'm usually drained, and that place is an escape. It's therapeutic, it recharges my batteries, I leave the worries behind.
转折Having said that, it was a bit of a letdown that the noise got in the way of what could've been a quiet evening.
收尾After two years of living near it, I can't imagine Baltimore without it. I'd definitely go back if I ever got another free weekend.
· 嘈杂地 / 拥挤地方:把 7 月 4 日那段拉长,收尾用 letdown
· 自然之地:去掉人群,I 块 + C 块
· 想再去的城市:主角换 Washington DC,一小时火车,博物馆免费
· 公共建筑:主角换 Peabody Library,B 块(专注)+ I 块
· 印象深刻的聚会 / 愉快夜晚:主角 = 烟火那晚,J 块 + C 块
· 不喜欢的短途旅行:每天去实验室的 shuttle,D 反面:挤、慢、没信号

4 · 相机与抖音

我自学剪辑 · 1000 万播放 · 哥哥 · 台球 · Part 2 第 3 8 12 14 16 18 19 20 29 30 32 33 题
开场I've got a real soft spot for making short videos. I taught myself editing in college, and my clips have reached over ten million views.
E 块It aligns with my interest. Every new shot sparks my curiosity and whets my appetite for more.
B 块Once I'm editing, I'm unaware of the passage of time. I don't want to be disturbed, and that guarantees my efficiency.
故事I remember one night I lost my camera bag on the subway in Beijing, and because of that, I spent three hours at the lost-and-found office. A stranger had handed it in.
K 块Most of my videos tell a small story with a twist, like a rollercoaster. People stay because they want to know the ending.
转折Having said that, scrolling through other people's videos is a total waste of time. I can lose an hour before I even notice.
收尾Now that I come to think of it, I probably shouldn't have spent so many nights editing. But I'll always remember it, because it taught me how to tell a story.
· 喜欢拍照的人:主角换成「我哥」,他拍,我剪,F 块
· 丢物品:故事段就是答案,拉长,D 块(方便:地铁 app 找回)
· 浪费时间:转折段拉成主体,G 反面
· 省时间的方法:用键盘快捷键和 AI 剪辑,D 块 + B 块
· 不喜欢的电影:K 块反面「predictable, clichéd」
· 读起来开心的书:How to Win Friends and Influence People,K 块 + C 块
· 送朋友的礼物:一个相机镜头,E 块 + J 块
· 好的购物服务:买相机时店员帮我试机两小时,D 块 + F 块
· 名人采访:看过李子柒或 Steve Jobs 采访,K 块 + F 块
· 不感兴趣的话:同事讲加密货币,我还是听完了,L 块 + 性格
· 运动员:丁俊晖,我和哥哥打台球,F 块 + A 块
