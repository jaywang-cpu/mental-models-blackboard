7.5 规矩

· 立场从开头到结尾不能变;每段第一句就是这段的观点
· 每段只讲一个理由,讲透:解释 + 例子 比 堆三个理由强
· 连接词每段最多 2 个(你过度用连接词,这会扣连贯分)
· 模板只是骨架,方括号里必须填题目自己的词;整句照搬无关模板,考官会不计分
· 大作文 260-300 词,小作文 160-190 词。拼不准的词换成会拼的,别冒险

1 · 合伙人 Moira

Moira · MAITONG 联合创始人 · 一起开了一家小的健康公司 · Part 2 第 1 4 6 7 9 11 27 31 题
开场The person who springs to mind is Moira, my co-founder. We met two years ago at Hopkins, in a startup class. She sat next to me, and we started talking about health, and that was it.
特征What stands out most is how organized she is. Every meeting has a plan, every plan has a deadline, and she never forgets anything. I'm the opposite, so we balance each other.
F 块She is affable and charismatic. When she talks, people listen, and she infects the whole team with her passion. Honestly, nobody is on a par with her.
故事I remember last winter, our first product failed after three months of testing. Everyone wanted to give up. She didn't. She said, okay, let's start again, and she stayed with me in the office every night until 2 a.m., eating cold noodles.
细节Three months later, the new version worked, and we got our first real customer. She cried a little that day. I did too, but I won't admit it.
A 块Those months really expanded my skill set. I learned how to plan, how to talk to people, how to keep going when things break. That sharpened my competitive edge and set me apart from others.
转折Having said that, she is not perfect. She is brutally honest. My first pitch came back covered in red, and she said, this is boring. She was right.
收尾Now that I think of it, I should have thanked her more often. If only every founder had a partner like her. I wouldn't be where I am without her.
· 教你新技能的人:故事改成「她教我做 pitch,第一次讲砸了五遍」
· 有条理的人:把「特征」那句拉长,讲她的会议记录
· 尊敬的年长者 / 困难成功的人:故事改成「投资人全拒了,她还是把产品做出来了」
· 印象深刻的课程:主角换成「Hopkins 的创业课,在那认识她」,故事不变
· 小时候学的技能:换成「我 5 岁学做饭」,A 块不变
· 重要决定:「和她一起开公司」就是那个决定

2 · 合伙人 Yvette

Yvette · MAITONG 联合创始人 · Part 2 第 2 5 10 13 15 21 题
开场Funny I should get this topic. I'm probably one of the least social people in the world, but I do know someone who is. That's Yvette, my other co-founder.
F 块She is such a larger than life character. She always has a story to tell and a joke to share. When she walks into a room, the room gets louder, in a good way.
相识We met at a startup event at Hopkins. To be honest, she didn't make much of an impression on me at first. I thought she was just loud. But the more we talked, the more I wanted to know her.
故事I remember one evening we sat in a café in Hangzhou until it closed. We talked for five hours about health, about family, about what we wanted to do with our lives. And because of that conversation, we decided to start MAITONG, a small health company.
细节The first year was hard. I saved money for twelve months and skipped a lot of dinners out just to pay for our first office. She did the same. We ate a lot of instant noodles.
J 块Working with her is a great bonding experience. We shoot the breeze, we share anecdotes, and the atmosphere is always vibrant and cordial, even on bad days.
转折Having said that, she is not easy to work with. She changes her mind fast, and sometimes I can't keep up.
收尾I wouldn't say she changed my life, but she definitely changed how I look at teamwork. After two years, I can't imagine building anything without her.

3 · 乌镇

家乡乌镇 · 西栅夜市 · 乌镇大剧院 · 杭州 · Part 2 第 17 22 23 24 25 26 28 34 题
开场Well, one place that's always stuck in my mind is the canal in Wuzhen, my hometown. It's a small water town, about five minutes' walk from my parents' house.
I 块I can feast my eyes on the landscape there. Old stone bridges, black roofs, white walls, and the water right below your feet. In the morning the light is soft and the whole street is quiet. It's a picturesque backdrop for photos and leisurely strolls.
故事I remember last Spring Festival, five of us from high school went to the night market on the west street to see the lanterns. On the way we got caught in the crowd, so by the time we found a spot, we could barely move. Someone stepped on my shoe. I lost my friend for twenty minutes.
细节But then the lanterns came on, hundreds of them, red and yellow, floating on the water. Everyone went quiet for a second. Then everyone took out their phones.
J 块We gathered together and enjoyed each other's company. We shot the breeze, shared old stories from school, and the ambience was electrifying, bustling with laughter.
C 块After months in the lab in the US, I'm usually drained, and going home is an escape. It's therapeutic, it recharges my batteries. I leave the worries behind.
转折Having said that, it was a bit of a letdown that the noise got in the way of what could've been a quiet evening. Next time I'll go at six in the morning.
收尾After twenty years of growing up there, I can't imagine my life without it. I'd definitely go back if I ever got another long holiday.

4 · 相机与抖音

我自学剪辑 · 1000 万播放 · 哥哥 · 台球 · Part 2 第 3 8 12 14 16 18 19 20 29 30 32 33 题
开场I've got a real soft spot for making short videos. I taught myself editing in college, with free tutorials on YouTube, and now my clips have reached over ten million views.
E 块It aligns with my interest. Every new shot sparks my curiosity and whets my appetite for more. I'll see a street corner and think, that's a shot.
B 块Once I'm editing, I'm unaware of the passage of time. I sit down at eight, I look up, and it's two in the morning. I don't want to be disturbed, and that guarantees my efficiency.
故事I remember one night I lost my camera bag on the bus to Hangzhou. I got off, I reached for it, and it wasn't there. My heart stopped. Because of that, I spent three hours at the lost-and-found office. In the end, a stranger had handed it in. I never found out who. I still think about that.
K 块Most of my videos tell a small story with a twist, like a rollercoaster. People stay because they want to know the ending. My brother shoots, I edit. He has the eye, I have the patience.
细节The one that got the most views was a thirty-second clip of my grandmother cooking. No music, no words, just her hands. Two million people watched it.
转折Having said that, scrolling through other people's videos is a total waste of time. I can lose an hour before I even notice. So I deleted the app from my phone, twice.
收尾Now that I think of it, I probably shouldn't have spent so many nights editing. But I'll always remember it, because it taught me how to tell a story.
