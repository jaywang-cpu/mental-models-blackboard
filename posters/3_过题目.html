写作

想不出理由时 · 6 维度 × 5 句

钱 Money
· Not only would this [政策/做法] ease the financial burden on [families], but it would also channel more money into [the local economy].
· Although the initial cost of [X] may seem considerable, it should be regarded as a long-term investment rather than an unnecessary expense.
· By [减少/提供 X], governments could free up public funds, which could in turn be spent on [education / healthcare].
· In the long run, the economic returns of [X] are likely to far outweigh the money spent on it.
· Without [X], many [low-income families] would be unable to afford [基本需要], which would further widen the gap between rich and poor.
时 Time
· By [using technology / working from home], people can save a considerable amount of time, thereby allowing them to focus on [what truly matters].
· While [X] may require extra effort at first, it enables people to work far more efficiently in the long run.
· Given that most people already have demanding schedules, [X] would help them strike a better balance between work and [family life].
· Rather than wasting hours on [commuting / paperwork], people could spend that time on [learning new skills].
· As a result, [人群] would be able to complete tasks in a fraction of the time, which would ultimately raise overall productivity.
健 Health
· Regular [exercise / outdoor activity] not only improves physical health but also alleviates stress, thus contributing to overall well-being.
· Given that [fast food / long working hours] is increasingly common, encouraging people to [X] would help them lead a healthier lifestyle.
· If [X] continues, it may place an enormous strain on the healthcare system, which would affect everyone in society.
· By [X], people are less likely to suffer from [obesity / anxiety], which would significantly improve their quality of life.
· Admittedly, [X] may bring short-term pleasure; however, its long-term impact on health cannot be ignored.
能 Skills
· Through [internships / hands-on projects], young people can develop practical skills that classroom learning alone cannot provide.
· Rather than merely memorising facts, students who [X] gain valuable experience in [teamwork / problem-solving].
· This experience would greatly improve their career prospects, since employers increasingly value [communication / adaptability].
· By stepping out of their comfort zone, [人群] are more likely to become independent and confident.
· Such skills are not only useful in the workplace but also equip people to deal with challenges in everyday life.
情 People
· Spending time [together / on shared activities] helps strengthen family bonds, which is particularly important in an era when [technology keeps people apart].
· By [studying abroad / volunteering], individuals learn to understand people from different backgrounds, which fosters tolerance and mutual respect.
· [X] encourages young people to develop a sense of responsibility towards [their community / society].
· Without [X], people may feel increasingly isolated, which could in turn lead to [loneliness / mental health problems].
· In this way, [X] brings people of different ages and cultures closer together, creating a more harmonious society.
环 Planet
· If governments invest in [public transport / renewable energy], it would substantially reduce air pollution while easing traffic congestion at the same time.
· Considering that natural resources are limited, it is essential that we [recycle / save energy] in order to protect the environment for future generations.
· [X] produces a large amount of waste and carbon emissions, which accelerates climate change.
· By choosing [local products / reusable items], individuals can make a small but meaningful contribution to protecting the natural environment.
· Although [X] may be convenient, its environmental cost is far too high to ignore.
